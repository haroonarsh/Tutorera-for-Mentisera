# IGCSE Physics Tutor in Dubai: How to Plan Lessons and Revision

**Suggested URL:** /blog/igcse-physics-tutor-dubai

IGCSE Physics can challenge students because success requires both conceptual understanding and accurate application of mathematics. Learners may memorise a formula but struggle to decide when it applies, interpret a graph or explain the physical meaning of a result. A suitable tutor should identify these differences and use evidence to plan lessons.

## Verify the syllabus and assessment route

Confirm the student's awarding body, syllabus code and examination series. Physics specifications may differ in content, assessment components and practical-skills arrangements. Obtain the current official syllabus and examination guidance before choosing textbooks or past papers. Avoid treating resources from different boards as interchangeable.

## Diagnose conceptual and numerical difficulties

Ask the learner to explain what a physical quantity means before calculating it. For example, a speed calculation requires understanding the relationship between distance and time, not just substitution into a formula. In electricity, learners may confuse current, potential difference and resistance even when they can reproduce an equation. A diagnostic task should include both explanation and calculation.

## Use diagrams and representations

Good Physics teaching moves between words, diagrams, graphs, equations and practical observations. When discussing motion, a tutor might ask the student to describe a distance-time graph and then connect its slope to a physical interpretation. When studying forces, clear diagrams can help separate the direction of forces from the direction of motion. Students should explain their reasoning rather than simply copy a completed diagram.

## Teach mathematical accuracy

Units, significant figures, rearranging equations and graph interpretation can affect marks across many topics. Maintain an error log distinguishing conceptual misunderstandings from algebra, arithmetic and unit conversion mistakes. Practise each weak skill in different contexts so that the learner can recognise when it applies.

## Include practical and investigative skills

Physics is not only a collection of calculations. Students may need to understand experimental design, measurements, sources of error and how conclusions follow from observations. A tutor should use safe demonstrations, diagrams or approved simulations where appropriate, while explaining the limits of a simulation. Specific practical assessment requirements must come from the student's current syllabus.

## Build a revision cycle

A manageable cycle includes recall of prior knowledge, focused explanation, independent questions and feedback. Add mixed-topic problems after the learner can handle individual topics. Reserve official past papers for checking application and examination timing. Analyse errors after each attempt rather than counting completed papers as the sole measure of progress.

## Choosing a Dubai tutor

Ask about direct experience with the student's specification, availability in the required time zone, lesson format, equipment and cancellation policy. Online sessions should support clear diagramming and worked calculations. For in-person lessons, verify the actual location and safeguarding arrangements. Check credentials using available evidence rather than relying on unverified claims.

## Measure improvement

Agree on a short target: correctly interpreting a motion graph, explaining a circuit concept or solving a set of unfamiliar numerical questions independently. Review written work after several sessions. Avoid grade guarantees and unsupported claims about tutor availability.
