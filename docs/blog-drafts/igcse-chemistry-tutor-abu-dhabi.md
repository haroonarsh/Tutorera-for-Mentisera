# IGCSE Chemistry Tutor in Abu Dhabi: Curriculum and Lesson Planning Guide

**Status:** Editorial draft — requires source verification and approval before publication.

**Suggested URL:** /blog/igcse-chemistry-tutor-abu-dhabi

Families in Abu Dhabi looking for IGCSE Chemistry support should evaluate how a tutor teaches concepts, calculations and practical reasoning. The right starting point is the learner's current syllabus and marked work, not a general promise of better grades.

## Confirm the exact course

Identify the awarding body, syllabus code and examination series. Assessment components and practical-skills arrangements may differ between specifications. Obtain the current official syllabus before selecting textbooks or past papers.

## Build a diagnostic picture

Ask the student to explain a recent mistake and solve a related question without help. Examine whether the difficulty involves scientific vocabulary, particle models, chemical equations, calculations or interpreting observations. An error in a multistep question may arise from a basic misunderstanding rather than the final calculation.

## Teach through multiple representations

Chemistry concepts become clearer when students connect written explanations, particle diagrams, equations and experimental observations. A tutor should explain why a representation is appropriate and ask the student to move between them. Memorising a definition without being able to apply it is an incomplete learning outcome.

## Practise calculations systematically

Work through units, ratios, formula rearrangement and relevant quantitative topics using the student's specification. Ask the learner to annotate each step and explain the result. Introduce unfamiliar questions only after the foundation is secure.

## Prepare for assessment

Use official specimen or past-paper questions to understand command words, mark schemes and time constraints. After marking, classify errors and assign focused practice. Avoid repeated full-paper practice without analysis. Check the current examination format before relying on older resources.

## Decide between online and in-person tuition

Online lessons require reliable audio, clear digital writing tools and a quiet learning space. In-person sessions may be preferable for some students, but location and safeguarding arrangements should be verified. For practical work, tutors should not imply that an online simulation automatically satisfies formal practical requirements.

## Agree on a four-week plan

Set a specific goal, such as explaining bonding models accurately or solving a class of calculations independently. Review work against the initial diagnostic assessment. Confirm lesson fees, currency, duration, cancellations and communication channels before committing.

## Choose with evidence

Ask about direct experience with the student's specification, feedback methods and how progress is measured. Prefer clear teaching and transparent terms over unsupported guarantees.
